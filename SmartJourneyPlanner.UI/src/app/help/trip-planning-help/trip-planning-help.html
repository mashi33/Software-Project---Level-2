Trip Planning Guide

Learn how to create, manage, edit, and invite travel companions to your trips easily.

1. How to Create a New Trip

  1. Log in to your Traveller Account and navigate to your Traveller Dashboard or the Create Trip section.
  2. Click on the "Create Trip" button.
  3. Fill in your trip details such as Destination, Start Date, End Date, and Budget.
  4. Click Save or Create to successfully generate your new travel itinerary.

2. How to Edit an Existing Trip

  1. Go to your trips list or dashboard where your created trips are displayed.
  2. Find the specific trip you want to modify and click the "Edit" button (or edit icon).
  3. Update the necessary details like dates, locations, or notes.
  4. Click Update to save your changes.

3. How to Invite Friends/Companions

  1. Open the specific trip details or itinerary page you have created.
  2. Look for the "Invite" or collaboration section.
  3. Enter your friend's registered email address or username.
  4. Send the invitation so they can view and collaborate on the trip plan together!
