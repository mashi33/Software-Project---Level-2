💰 My Budget Help

Learn how to manage your travel expenses and stay within budget.

📊 Understanding Budget Management

The My Budget feature helps you track expenses, set spending limits, and monitor your travel costs in real-time. This ensures you stay within your planned budget and avoid overspending during your trips.

🎯 Setting Up Your Budget

1

Create a Trip

Start by creating a new trip in the Trip Planner section. Each trip can have its own budget.

2

Set Budget Limit

When creating or editing a trip, specify your total budget amount. This will be your spending limit.

3

Track Progress

Use the budget dashboard to monitor your spending against the set limit.

➕ Adding Expenses

To add expenses to your budget:

  1. Navigate to the "My Budget" section from the main menu
  2. Click the "Add Expense" button
  3. Select the trip you want to add the expense to
  4. Enter expense details:
    • Expense name (e.g., "Hotel", "Food", "Transportation")
    • Amount spent
    • Category
    • Date of expense
    • Description (optional)
  5. Click "Save" to add the expense

📈 Budget Status Indicators

✅

On Track

Your spending is within 70% of your budget limit. You're doing great!

⚠️

Near Limit

Your spending is between 70-90% of your budget. Consider reducing expenses.

🚨

Over Budget

You've exceeded your budget limit. Review your expenses and adjust spending.

✏️ Managing Expenses

Editing Expenses

Go to the budget dashboard, find the expense you want to edit, and click the edit button. Update the details and save changes.

Deleting Expenses

To remove an expense, click the delete button next to it in the budget dashboard. Confirm the deletion to permanently remove it from your records.

📊 Budget Dashboard Features

  • Visual Progress Bars: See at a glance how much of your budget you've used
  • Expense Breakdown: View expenses by category (food, transport, accommodation, etc.)
  • Filtering: Filter expenses by date, category, or trip
  • Sorting: Sort expenses by amount, date, or category
  • Total Summary: See total spent, remaining budget, and percentage used

💡 Budget Tips

💡

Set realistic budgets based on destination costs and travel duration

💡

Track expenses daily to avoid surprises at the end of your trip

💡

Keep a buffer of 10-15% for unexpected expenses

💡

Use categories to identify spending patterns and adjust future budgets

❓ Common Issues

Why can't I see my budget?

Make sure you've created a trip and set a budget limit. Budgets are tied to specific trips.

Can I change my budget limit?

Yes, edit your trip details to update the budget limit at any time.

What if I accidentally delete an expense?

Deleted expenses cannot be recovered. Be careful when deleting and double-check before confirming.
