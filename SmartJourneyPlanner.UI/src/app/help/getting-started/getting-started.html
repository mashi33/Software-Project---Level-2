Getting Started with Smart Journey Planner

Learn how to create an account and begin planning your journeys easily.

1. How to Sign Up (Create an Account)

  1. Click on the Sign Up button located at the top right corner of the navigation bar or on the landing page.
  2. Select your user type: whether you are registering as a Traveller or a Provider.
  3. Fill in your required details accurately (Username, Email, and Password).
  4. Click the Register button to submit. Once successful, you will be automatically redirected to the login page.

2. How to Login

  1. Navigate to the Login page.
  2. Enter your registered Email and Password.
  3. Click the Login button.
  4. Based on your account type (Traveller/Provider), the system will successfully take you to your dedicated Dashboard.
