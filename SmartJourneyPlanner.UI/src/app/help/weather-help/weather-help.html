☀️ Weather Search Help

Learn how to check the forecast, get packing tips, and plan your day in Sri Lanka.

🌤️ Understanding Weather Search

WeatherWise helps you check the weather for any location in Sri Lanka. Search a city, pick a date, and get current conditions, hourly details, a 7-day outlook, plus smart packing, outfit, and activity suggestions tailored to the forecast.

🔍 Searching for Weather

1

Enter a Location

Type a city or place name in Sri Lanka (e.g. Colombo, Galle, Ella, Kandy).

2

Select a Date

Use the date picker or the calendar on the left to choose the day you want to check.

3

Fetch Weather

Click Fetch Weather or press Enter. Results appear on the right.

If several places match your search, a dropdown will appear so you can pick the correct one. Only locations inside Sri Lanka are supported.

Location Search Tips

  1. Enter at least 3 letters of a valid place name.
  2. As you type, matching locations in Sri Lanka may appear in a dropdown.
  3. Click a result to load its weather immediately.
  4. Your recent searches are saved and shown in the sidebar for quick access.
  5. The last city you searched is remembered so it can load automatically next time.

📅 Choosing a Date

You can check weather for today or future dates:

  • Date picker — Select any date in the search card.
  • Inline calendar — Click a day in the calendar to switch the forecast.
  • Month navigation — Use the ‹ › arrows to change months.

Forecast data is typically available up to about 16 days ahead. Choosing a date too far in the future will show a warning.

🌡️ Temperature Unit

Switch between °C and °F using the toggle in the search card. Your preference is saved and applied to current temperature, hourly values, and the 7-day forecast.

📊 Understanding the Results

Current Weather

Shows the selected city’s average temperature, condition (Sunny / Cloudy / Rainy), humidity, wind speed, and precipitation for the chosen date.

Advisory Banner

😎

Clear & Bright

Great outdoor day — sunscreen and water recommended.

⛅

Cloudy Skies

Mostly overcast. Light layers are a good idea.

☔

Grab an Umbrella

Rain or storms possible — carry rain protection.

Hourly Forecast

See temperature and rain chance across the day (morning to night), plus a temperature trend line.

7-Day Outlook

Scroll through the next days to compare temperature, condition, and humidity. Use the left/right arrows to move the cards.

🎒 Smart Suggestions

Based on temperature and weather condition, the app suggests:

  • Packing List — What to bring (umbrella, sunscreen, light jacket, etc.)
  • Outfit — Recommended clothing for the day
  • Activities — Good things to do in the current conditions

These tips update automatically when you change the city or date.

❓ Common Questions

Why does it say “Location not found in Sri Lanka”?

Weather Search only supports places inside Sri Lanka. Make sure the name is spelled correctly and is a real city or landmark on the island.

Why do I see a dropdown instead of weather?

When several places match your text, the app shows a list so you can choose the right one. Select a location to load its weather.

Can I check weather more than 16 days ahead?

No. Forecast data is limited to roughly 16 days into the future. Choose an earlier date if you see a date warning.

Why is there no hourly or 7-day data sometimes?

Some dates or locations may temporarily lack detailed hourly or multi-day data from the weather service. Try today or another nearby date.

Does the app remember my last search?

Yes. Your last searched city and temperature unit preference are saved locally so the next visit can restore them.
